Privacy Policy

Lost Wonders Rebuilt and its publishing tool, Shorts Launcher. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What Shorts Launcher is

Shorts Launcher is a private desktop application used only by the owner of the Lost Wonders Rebuilt channel to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It runs on the owner's computer. It has no website, no server and no other users.

What data it accesses

When the owner connects an account, the application requests only the access it needs:

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly): to upload and schedule the channel's videos, and to read the channel's name and ID so the app can show which channel is connected.
  • TikTok and Instagram: to publish the channel's videos and read the account name.

It does not read, collect or store anything about viewers, subscribers or other people.

How data is stored and shared

Login tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to the platform the video is being published to (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta/Instagram). No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Shorts Launcher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google), or in the TikTok and Instagram app settings. Removing the app's local files deletes all stored tokens.
